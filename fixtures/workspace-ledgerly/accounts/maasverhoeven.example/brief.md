# Maas & Verhoeven Foodservice — account brief

## Snapshot
Maas & Verhoeven Foodservice is a family-owned B2B food wholesaler in Ghent supplying around 6,000 restaurants and independent retailers from three depots, with approximately 310 employees.[^1]

## Business & offering
Broadline foodservice wholesale for restaurants and independent retailers, working with about 1,200 active suppliers.[^1]

## Situation & triggers
- 2026-07-01 — acquired Delcourt Traiteur in Liège, its ninth acquisition since 2015; purchasing and administration will be integrated over the coming year.[^2]
- 2026-09-10 — launched Buurtmarkt with a national retailer, growing from 10 pilot stores to 100.[^3]
- Careers page lists 14 open positions in warehouse, delivery and sales, and no vacancies in finance or administration.[^4]
