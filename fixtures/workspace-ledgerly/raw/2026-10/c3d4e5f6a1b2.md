Ghent wholesaler Maas & Verhoeven has acquired Delcourt Traiteur in Liège, its ninth acquisition since 2015. The deal closed on 1 July 2026. Finance director Sofie Claes said the group will integrate Delcourt's purchasing and administration over the coming year.
