# Outreach (drafts only — never sent by the engine)

- **Trigger → insight → ask**: a true, recent trigger; an insight that shows you understand its consequence; a low-friction,
  interest-based ask ("worth a look?" over "30 minutes Tuesday?").
- **PAS** for problem-aware personas: Problem → Agitate (cost of inaction, with a number if sourced) → Solution (one line).
- Under 100 words for a first email; one idea per email; no attachments; subject ≤ 5 words, lowercase reads human.
- LinkedIn connection note ≤ 300 characters, no pitch. Follow-up DM after acceptance.
- Warm intro: write the forwardable blurb for the connector — two sentences they can paste.
- Voice: follow `company/voice/` (derived from the team's own emails) and `learnings/preferences.md`.
- Every claim about the prospect must be in their brief with a citation.
