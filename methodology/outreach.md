# Outreach (drafts only — never sent by the engine)

- **Trigger → insight → ask**: one true, specific, dated trigger; an insight that connects it to a pain the persona has
  (from the ICP); a low-friction, interest-based ask ("worth a look?" over "30 minutes Tuesday?"). One idea per message.
- **PAS** for problem-aware personas: Problem → Agitate (cost of inaction, with a number if sourced) → Solution (one line).
- **Freshness:** a trigger older than ~90 days gets re-checked first; never congratulate someone on news that's been
  overtaken.
- **Channel follows the best way in:**
  - warm path → an intro request: a short note to the team member (why this account, why now) plus a 2–3 sentence
    blurb the connector can forward;
  - trigger or cold → a first email (≤ 100 words, no attachments, subject ≤ 5 words, lowercase reads human) and/or a
    LinkedIn connection note (≤ 300 characters, no pitch) with a follow-up DM after acceptance.
- **Voice:** follow `company/voice/` (derived from the team's own emails) and `learnings/preferences.md`.
- **Every claim about the prospect** is in their brief with a citation, and the draft footnotes it. Never invent
  familiarity, numbers or mutual connections.
