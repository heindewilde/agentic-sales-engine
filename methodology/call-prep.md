# Call prep

One page, built from the account brief, scores, people and notes.

1. **Snapshot** — who they are, why now (triggers), why us (fit factors with highest levels).
2. **Who's in the room** — each attendee: role in deal, likely priorities, how we're connected.
3. **Hypotheses to test** — from low-confidence or unknown factors and open questions.
4. **Discovery questions (SPIN)** — Situation (only what research couldn't answer), Problem, Implication, Need-payoff.
5. **Qualification gaps (MEDDICC)** — Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain,
   Champion, Competition: what we know (cited), what we must learn on this call.
6. **Likely objections** — with evidence-based responses (case studies from `company/materials/`).
7. **Desired outcome and next step.**
