# Ways in

A "way in" is a concrete, defensible reason a specific person would take the first conversation.

## Warm paths (strongest first)
1. Team member directly connected to a target persona.
2. Team member connected to someone else at the account who can introduce (ask for the intro, don't name-drop).
3. Shared former employer or school with the target persona, recent overlap.
4. Mutual customer, investor or partner.
Strength also depends on recency (`connected_on`) and on whether the member actually knows the person — ask the member.

## Trigger hooks
Tie the opener to a dated event from `signals/`: "Congrats on opening the Ghent plant — teams scaling a second site
usually hit X." The hook must be true, recent, cited, and connected to the problem you solve.

## Shared context
Same event or conference, the person's own talk/podcast/article, a community both belong to. Use public professional
content only.

Output in the brief's **Ways in**: ranked list, each with path/hook, member involved, source, and a one-line suggested opener.
